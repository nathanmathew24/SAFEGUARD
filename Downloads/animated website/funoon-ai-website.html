The Process

Six Steps.
Zero Missed Leads.

From first WhatsApp message to scored, qualified lead ready for your team — Funoon AI handles every step automatically, around the clock.

01
Lead Capture
Every message. Captured instantly.

Every inbound message — WhatsApp, Instagram DM, or web form — is captured instantly, 24/7. No lead slips through, no matter the hour.

02
AI Qualification
Natural conversation. Real intelligence.

Wasel converses naturally in Arabic, English, and Hinglish — extracting budget, model preference, timeline, and purchase intent from every buyer.

03
Lead Scoring
Know who to call first.

Each lead is scored automatically: HOT — ready to buy, WARM — researching, COLD — early stage. Your team knows exactly who to call first.

04
Auto Follow-Up
No lead left behind.

Warm and cold leads receive timed follow-up messages automatically — no manual chasing. Wasel re-engages until the buyer is ready or opts out.

05
Inventory Matching
Stock answers in seconds.

Wasel cross-references your live stock in real time. When a buyer names a model and budget, it surfaces exact matches — photos, specs, availability — instantly.

06
Live Intelligence Dashboard
Full visibility. Zero setup needed.

Every lead, score, and conversation syncs to your live dashboard and Google Sheet automatically. Full visibility — no CRM setup required.
