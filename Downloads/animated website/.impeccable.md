## Design Context

### Users
UAE car dealership owners (non-technical, very busy). They receive a word-of-mouth recommendation or see an ad, land on this page on a laptop during or after business hours, and need to quickly understand the ROI before booking a demo. They are the decision-maker and the budget holder. They care about one thing: not losing deals. The site must convey authority and credibility instantly — if it looks like a generic SaaS template, they bounce.

### Brand Personality
**Three words**: urgent · authoritative · Gulf-built

Funoon.ai is not a chatbot company — it's a sales weapon for car dealerships. It should feel like the instrument panel of a premium vehicle: precise, purposeful, every detail deliberate. Not cold tech. Not playful startup. Confident authority that understands the Gulf market.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Theme**: Dark, warm. Not cool-dark (no cyan, no neon blue). The warm cream accent (#fff3ea territory) against near-black is the core contrast. WhatsApp green is used sparingly — it's a signal color, not a brand color.

**Reference**: plug.ae — surprising layout scale, editorial boldness, not another SaaS template.

**Anti-references**: No generic SaaS (Linear-style dark with teal accents). No glassmorphism. No identical card grids with emoji icons. No Inter font.

**Typography system**:
- Display/headlines: `Big Shoulders Display` (wght 800-900) — condensed industrial, like the stencil on a car chassis. Commands authority. Unexpected in B2B SaaS.
- Body: `Lexend Deca` (wght 300-400) — designed for readability, clean without being generic.

**Color tokens (OKLCH)**:
- `--bg`: `oklch(5.5% 0.005 58)` — near-black with a breath of warm amber
- `--bg2`: `oklch(9% 0.006 58)` — surfaces
- `--surface2`: `oklch(13.5% 0.007 58)` — elevated surfaces
- `--gold`: `oklch(95% 0.018 78)` — the warm cream accent (was #fff3ea)
- `--wa`: `oklch(72% 0.19 145)` — WhatsApp green

### Design Principles
1. **Scale is the surprise** — headlines should fill the viewport. Larger than expected, bolder than comfortable. The size itself communicates confidence.
2. **Dark and warm, never cold** — this is a Gulf product. The warmth of the cream palette is intentional and culturally resonant. Never introduce blue or cool tones.
3. **Data leads conviction** — the stats (67%, 6h+, 78%) are more persuasive than feature lists. Let them breathe, scale them up, let them be the visual anchor.
4. **Every transition earns its smoothness** — use `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)` (ease-out-expo) as default. Never `ease` or `linear`.
5. **Automotive precision** — the same care that goes into a luxury car's dashboard goes into every detail here. No orphans, no rounding inconsistencies, no pixel-off alignment.
